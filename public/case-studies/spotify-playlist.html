Spotify Study Playlist Optimizer

A web and mobile tool that generates personalized study playlists based on listening habits and skip patterns to help students and professionals stay focused.

Product Overview

The Spotify Study Playlist Optimizer is designed to enhance focus during study or work sessions by generating adaptive playlists tailored to individual listening behavior. It combines personalization and analytics to sustain engagement and productivity.

Project Team

Solo PM (Portfolio Project)

  • Research & Competitive Analysis
  • MVP Design & Prototype
  • Metrics & A/B Testing
  • Roadmap & Iteration

Problem Space

Students and professionals often struggle to find study music that keeps them focused. Generic playlists can be distracting, while curating custom playlists is time-consuming.

  • Distracting songs break concentration
  • Finding or building the right playlist takes time
  • Engagement drops when music doesn’t match user preferences

User Research & Insights

Hypothesis: Personalization in music selection can improve focus and engagement.

Methods:

  • Surveys on study habits and music preferences
  • Interviews with students and working professionals
  • Competitive analysis of Spotify Focus playlists, Brain.fm, and Lo-fi YouTube channels

Findings:

  • Existing playlists are static and not responsive to listener behavior
  • Users desire playlists that adapt to their skip patterns and listening context

Market & Competitive Analysis

Competitor Strengths Weaknesses Opportunity
Spotify Focus Curated playlists, large reach Static, non-adaptive Personalization based on skips and listening history
Brain.fm Scientifically designed for focus Paid, limited integration with other platforms Free, adaptive solution integrated with Spotify
Lo-fi Channels Engaging, popular with students No personalization or feedback tracking Add adaptive, metrics-driven playlist generation

Solution Concept

Product Vision: Provide adaptive, data-driven study playlists that optimize focus and productivity.

MVP Features:

  • Connect Spotify account
  • Generate playlists optimized for tempo and mood
  • Track skip patterns and session engagement

Future Roadmap:

  • Mood-based recommendations
  • Collaborative study playlists
  • Integration with calendar and study sessions

A/B Testing & Experimentation

Hypothesis: Adaptive playlists increase engagement and focus.

  • Control: Static playlist
  • Variant A: Personalized playlist based on listening history
  • Variant B: Personalized + real-time skip adaptation

Metrics:

  • Average session length
  • Skip rate
  • Playlist reuse
  • User focus satisfaction (survey)

Expected Outcome: Determine which variant maximizes engagement and sustained focus.

Metrics & Success Criteria

  • Playlist completion rate
  • Average listening time per session
  • Playlist reuse rate
  • User-reported focus improvement

Reflection

  • Identified key pain points in study and listening workflows
  • Developed an adaptive solution MVP for Spotify users
  • Planned structured A/B experiments to measure engagement impact
  • Defined clear engagement and focus metrics for success
  • Outlined future roadmap for enhanced personalization and collaboration
